# Frontier life and folklore

User direction, September 16, 2026: original insect livestock inspired by the broad idea of alien cattle; small biomass-eating pests called tibbles; unsettling, sometimes absurd space stories with the atmosphere of station disasters and early space-race folklore. Existing fiction is inspiration for roles and tone, not a source of names, art, characters, or copied lore.

All space-race-style stories in this game are fictional. Do not present disputed historical rumors as established history.

## In the first playable build

**Bristlebacks** are six-legged grazers with mineral plates, broad ground-combing feelers, and a warm abdomen. A small herd wanders outside the colony, eats lichen, and moves on when a patch is exhausted. Their silhouettes, names, colors, and descriptions are original. Initial handling, trust, feeding and nutrient-curd collection are implemented through [husbandry posts](husbandry.md). [Fenced pastures](pastures.md), crew gates and containment readings are implemented. [Herd reproduction](breeding.md) now adds optional pairings, carried broods, parentage and growing hatchlings. Genetics and broader veterinary/product systems remain unfinished.

**Tibbles** are tiny translucent seed-mites with hinged mouthparts. The first successful satellite salvage brings one home in the cargo. Tibbles seek food stores or hydroponics, eat biomass, damage crops, and reproduce when well fed. Their population is bounded in the prototype. Biofilter traps catch nearby tibbles using a food lure. The game reports introductions, losses, reproduction, and containment. Broader biomass targets such as fibers, corpses, compost, and organic insulation are planned as those materials enter the simulation.

**The voice on channel zero** is a short original signal encounter. An obsolete rescue beacon repeats a landing clearance with an impossible date. The player may isolate it or spend a component decoding its coordinates. Decoding reveals a fuel cache and unsettles the crew enough to reduce their energy. This is a small authored event, not yet a procedural mystery engine.

## Future possibilities, not implemented

- A vacuum-rated seed vault that grows roots through insulation whenever its owner sleeps.
- An inspection satellite that keeps issuing maintenance citations to a colony demolished centuries ago.
- A rescue capsule that appears in old manifests before it is manufactured.
- Bristleback herds that stop clicking in unison before an otherwise invisible event.
- A calibration tape that gives correct measurements for the next room rather than the current room.
- A discarded experiment whose harmless-looking microbial film eats suit seals.

Keep these encounters small, observable, and consequential. Give players warnings, a way to investigate, and practical containment. Let strange stories emerge from ordinary logistics and maintenance rather than making every discovery a scripted catastrophe.
