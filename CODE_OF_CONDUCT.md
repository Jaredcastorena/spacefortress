# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, patient, and specific. Welcome people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and code without attacking the person behind them.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private information, and sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Where it applies

This code applies in the repository, issue tracker, review discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing SPACEFORTRESS.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through a non-public contact method on their Git hosting profile. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal details.

The maintainer may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this code. Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.
